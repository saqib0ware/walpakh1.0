Details that depend on your journey

Hotel names depend on group size, dates and availability. Adventure activities, camping, access and attraction operation remain subject to season, weather, permissions, safety conditions and local requirements.

  • Gurez, Keran, Bangus and other sensitive or border destinations require current access, road-condition and permission confirmation.
  • Camping is only at authorised or permitted campsites and follows local environmental and waste-management rules.
  • Visa and entry requirements vary by nationality for international journeys.
